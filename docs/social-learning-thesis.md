# Social-learning experience thesis

Working concept: **SkillCity** — a cooperative city-roleplay game where competence creates status.

## Core session

A 6–8 minute city shift assigns optional roles such as driver, dispatcher, shopkeeper, courier, traffic officer or responder. The city generates practical incidents. Players earn reputation, cosmetics and collection progress by solving them cleanly.

Examples:
- A school-zone incident rewards correct speed/sign behavior and cooperative crossing management.
- A delivery job requires mental arithmetic for change, weight, fuel or route choices.
- An emergency scene requires dispatcher + driver communication and correct priority decisions.
- A market rush makes players price, budget and route limited stock.

## Why this borrows from winners without cloning them

- Roleplay sandbox: social self-expression and player-authored stories.
- Cozy collection/progression: persistent status and low-pressure return loop.
- Round/event structure: frequent reset points and shareable moments.
- Applied-learning fantasy: knowledge is an instrument for status and success, not a worksheet.

## Monetization

Prioritize identity/status and convenience over competence:
- cosmetic vehicle/body kits,
- home/garage decoration,
- emotes/role animations,
- additional cosmetic loadout slots,
- themed city seasons,
- private server controls that do not create public-server advantage.

Never sell the correct answer, basic safety rules, or unfair job power.
